Job Tracker - Never Lose Track of Your Job Applications
Are you tired of losing track of which jobs you've applied to? Job Tracker is a privacy-focused Chrome extension that helps you organize your job search with local, offline storage.
KEY FEATURES:
🎯 Auto-Fill Job Details
Visit any job posting on LinkedIn, Indeed, or 20+ other job sites, and Job Tracker automatically extracts the company name, job title, and job URL. No more copy-pasting!
💾 Local Storage - Your Privacy Matters
All your data stays on YOUR computer. We use browser IndexedDB technology - no external servers, no cloud sync, no tracking. Your job search data is yours alone.
📊 Powerful Management Features
- Track application status (Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected, On Hold)
- Add recruiter contact information
- Record response remarks and notes
- Search and filter your entire job history
- Country-based organization for international job searches
📥 Excel Export
Export your job applications to Excel (.xlsx) format for:
- Sharing with career counselors
- Creating backup copies
- Advanced analysis in spreadsheet software
- Importing to other tools
👥 Multi-User Support
Perfect for households with multiple job seekers. Each family member can have their own profile with separate tracking.
🎨 Accessibility First
- Light, Dark, and High Contrast themes
- Keyboard navigation support
- Clean, modern interface
🌐 Supported Job Sites (22+):
LinkedIn, Indeed (UK/Germany/Ireland), TotalJobs, Reed, CWJobs, Jobsite, Fish4Jobs, Adzuna, StepStone, Monster, XING, EURES, and more!
